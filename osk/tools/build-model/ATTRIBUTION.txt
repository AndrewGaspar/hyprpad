hyprpad-osk English prediction model.

Unigram frequencies from wordfreq (large_en), Robyn Speer et al.,
https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq — data licensed CC BY-SA 4.0.
Word validity and casing from SCOWL / Hunspell en_US as distributed with
LibreOffice, https://github.com/LibreOffice/dictionaries — MIT-like licence.
Bigram successor counts from the Leipzig Corpora Collection (English news),
Universitat Leipzig, https://wortschatz.uni-leipzig.de/ — CC BY 4.0.
